## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising vision of modern masculine luxury, artisanal precision, and quiet confidence. Designed for discerning clientele acquiring bespoke leather goods, horological accessories, fine jewelry, and tailored hardware, the interface establishes an atmosphere of an intimate private showroom. 

The aesthetic style merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Tactile Modern Architecture**:
- **Tone & Mood**: Monolithic, deliberate, commanding, and impeccably finished. The interface prioritizes restraint, allowing physical materials—full-grain leather, brushed bronze, titanium, and obsidian slate—to lead visually through generous negative space and surgical alignment.
- **Visual Tension**: Deep matte obsidian fields paired with precise, micro-weight borders and warm brushed gold accents create high-end retail prestige without visual clutter or ostentatious gloss.
- **Interaction Ethos**: Transitions are measured and deliberate (ease-out curves, subtle luminance shifts, zero bouncy spring physics). Every micro-interaction communicates durability, weight, and timeless value.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in deep, light-absorbent volcanic blacks and structured charcoals, punctuated exclusively by luminous brushed bronze, gold, and champagne metallic tones.

### Obsidian Core & Surface Scale
- **Canvas Base (`#0F1115`)**: Deep obsidian base for full-screen viewports; non-reflective and absorptive.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#171922`)**: Structural cards, navigation bars, sheet drawers, and product containers.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#212430`)**: Elevated dropdowns, active item highlights, and input field backgrounds.
- **Micro-Border Obsidian (`#2D313E`)**: Architectural hair-thin dividers, structural framing, and inactive element outlines.

### Precious Accent Architecture
- **Primary Aureate Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: Focal actions, key indicators, active selection rings, and high-value highlights.
- **Burnished Bronze (`#C59B27`)**: Hover states, micro-emboss borders, and secondary metadata iconography.
- **Champagne Light (`#E5C07B`)**: High-contrast text highlights, promotional tags, and fine jewelry details.

### Editorial Neutrals & Text Tiers
- **Text Crisp Primary (`#E6E8EC`)**: Headlines, product titles, and critical pricing numerals.
- **Text Muted Slate (`#9CA3AF`)**: Editorial copy, technical specs, breadcrumbs, and inactive controls.
- **Text Subtle Charcoal (`#5A6072`)**: Legal disclaimers, SKU numbers, and subtle watermark metadata.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between heritage editorial authority and technical contemporary precision. 

- **Display & High-Level Editorial**: Handled by **Playfair Display**. Used for hero statements, capsule collection titles, and premium price calls. The italic variant may be applied sparingly to single connecting words in editorial titles (e.g., *The* Autumn Solstice *Edition*).
- **Functional Interface & Technical Narrative**: Powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Engineered for maximum legibility in product detail matrices, specifications, checkout flows, and operational micro-copy.
- **Pricing & Currency Representation**: Always format the Indian Rupee symbol with clean vertical metric balance: `₹ 2,45,000`. In large luxury cards, the currency glyph uses medium weight at 85% scale relative to the integer numerals.
- **Uppercase Kerning Rule**: Any label or button text rendered in all-caps (`label-caps`) must maintain between `0.12em` and `0.16em` letter-spacing to preserve architectural elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a structured 12-column architectural grid on desktop devices, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Horizontal Framing**: Desktop viewports constrain central catalog grids to a maximum width of `1440px` with minimum outer canvas gutters of `margin-desktop`.
- **Vertical Hierarchy**: Generous vertical cadences (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) govern the spacing between distinct product showcases, atelier workshops, and editorial lookbooks. Elements within a product block remain tightly bound (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to communicate structural unity.
- **Fluid Structural Alignment**: Micro-borders strictly trace the column grid. Cards connect via single pixel border overlaps rather than floating disconnectedly, resembling bespoke display vitrines.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain an atmosphere of matte refinement, floating drop shadows are discarded in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Micro-Borders**.

- **Surface Tiering**:
  - `Base Floor (#0F1115)`: System background.
  - `Tier 1 Raised (#171922)`: Product cards, filter sidebars, and navigational elements.
  - `Tier 2 Floating (#212430)`: Modal dialogs, drawer trays, fly-out cart, and search autocomplete menus.
- **Micro-Border Precision**: All surface boundaries are demarcated by a 1px solid stroke in `#2D313E`. Active or selected elements elevate through a border color change to `#D4AF37` or a fine linear gradient from `#D4AF37` to `#2D313E`.
- **Ambient Bronzed Luminescence**: Floating modals and drawer elements project an ambient dark glow rather than a grey shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Glass Details**: The primary sticky navigation uses a subtle backdrop blur: background color `rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(45, 49, 62, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design system is unapologetically **Sharp (Level 0)**. 

- **Geometry**: The default corner radius is strictly `0px` across buttons, inputs, product cards, popovers, badges, and modals. 
- **Design Intent**: Razor-sharp corners mirror the architectural cuts of luxury watches, precision-machined metal hardware, and hand-cut leather edges. 
- **Exceptions**: Subtle circular shapes (`border-radius: 50%`) are reserved exclusively for color-swatch selectors, avatar rings, and carousel bullet navigation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action (Buy Now / Add to Atelier Cart)**: Solid brushed gold (`#D4AF37`) background, text in `#0F1115` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 12px, weight 700, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em). 0px border radius, height 48px. Hover shifts background to `#E5C07B` with smooth 200ms transition.
- **Secondary Action (Explore Collection / View Details)**: Background transparent, 1px solid `#2D313E` border, text in `#E6E8EC`. Hover state elevates the border to 1px solid `#D4AF37` with subtle metallic color shift.
- **Ghost Action**: Background transparent, no border. Text in `#9CA3AF` with an underlined hover state rendered via a 1px gold bottom stroke.

### Badges & Status Tags
- **Curated / Exclusive**: 1px border `#C59B27`, background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`, text `#E5C07B`, uppercase 10px, weight 700, letter-spacing 0.16em, padding: 4px 8px.
- **Offer / Privilege Tag**: Background `#212430`, border `1px solid #2D313E`, right-hand side highlighted with a 2px vertical gold strip. Text contains the promotional discount value in `#E6E8EC`.
- **Stock / Status (e.g., 'Only 2 Left', 'Bespoke Order')**: Small dot indicator (4px square or circle) colored in `#D4AF37` followed by `#9CA3AF` typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls**: Background `#171922`, 1px solid `#2D313E`, text `#E6E8EC`, height 48px, padding 0 16px. Focused state transitions border to `#D4AF37` with zero outer ring glow.
- **Floating Labels**: 11px uppercase (`#9CA3AF`), pinned directly above or within the upper boundary of the input box.

### Product Vitrine Cards
- **Structure**: Sharp 0px corners, background `#171922`, framed with a 1px border `#2D313E`.
- **Visual Presentation**: Ratio 4:5 or 1:1 image area with neutral dark grey staging background (`#12141B`).
- **Product Title**: Displayed in `title-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Playfair Display`), single-line truncation.
- **Price Tag Line**: Shows `₹ 18,500` format using `price-md`. Strikethrough pricing rendered in `#5A6072` with standard typography.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction**: Image scales subtly (1.03x) within fixed overflow over 400ms, bottom micro-bar reveals "Quick Acquire" drawer.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 18px square box, 1px border `#2D313E`, background `#0F1115`. Checked state features solid fill `#D4AF37` with a bespoke `#0F1115` checkmark icon.
- **Attribute Pickers (Leather, Ring Size, Strap Width)**: Segmented tiles with sharp 0px corners, 1px border `#2D313E`, background transparent. Active selection features a solid `#D4AF37` border and pure `#E6E8EC` typography.
